
AI-Powered Hospital Readmission Reduction
Overview
Hospital readmissions are a major challenge for healthcare organizations because they can negatively affect patient outcomes and increase healthcare costs.

This project explores how artificial intelligence and predictive analytics can help identify patients at risk for hospital readmission.

Objective
To demonstrate how healthcare organizations can use patient information and predictive analytics to improve care and reduce avoidable readmissions.

Readmission Workflow
Patient Data ↓ Risk Factor Analysis ↓ AI Prediction Model ↓ High-Risk Patient Alert ↓ Care Management Intervention ↓ Reduced Readmissions

Common Risk Factors
Advanced age
Diabetes
Hypertension
Multiple medications
Previous admissions
Lack of follow-up care
Benefits
Improved patient outcomes
Reduced healthcare costs
Better care coordination
Earlier intervention
Data-driven decision-making
Challenges
Data privacy
Data quality
Algorithm bias
Model transparency
Conclusion
AI can support healthcare teams by identifying high-risk patients and helping providers intervene before readmissions occur.
